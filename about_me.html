My Reflections & Goals

My Current Reality

My existing profile bio covers the "beast" part—the relentless energy and high standards. But the true bio is simpler: I'm a frontend developer driven by pure spite against inefficient layout and poor UX. My silence is not aloofness; it's intense focus. I turn caffeine into clean, semantic markup.

Goals in the Frontend Wizards Program

  • Mastering advanced accessibility patterns (ARIA, keyboard navigation, screen reader testing).
  • Becoming fluent in complex CSS Grid/Flexbox interactions for highly dynamic, responsive interfaces.
  • Building robust, highly performant web applications that could run on a literal potato.

Areas of Low Confidence

While I can brute-force almost any layout, deep-dive performance optimization, especially around bundle splitting and complex Webpack/Rollup configurations, sometimes feels like fighting a black hole. Also, keeping up with the JavaScript framework churn is a full-time job in itself.

A Note to Future Self

Hey, Draq. Remember this feeling—the grind. Never forget the simple truth: you write code for users, not compilers. Don't be too much of a Tsukishima; occasionally, share your knowledge. And for the love of all that is holy, stick to one framework for more than six months.

Extra Thoughts

The "love of my life" hobby is an ongoing ticket. If anyone finds a human who appreciates a beautifully centered modal and commits at 3 AM, please submit a pull request.
